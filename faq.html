FAQ

What is probate court?
Probate court handles matters like guardianships, conservatorships, and trust or estate disputes.
How can I avoid probate court?
You can avoid probate court by using estate planning tools like living trusts, joint ownership, and beneficiary designations.
What is guardianship abuse?
Guardianship abuse occurs when a guardian exploits, neglects, or financially drains the person they are appointed to protect.
Does guardianship mean total loss of rights?
No. Many individuals are eligible for limited guardianships or supported decision-making, which allow them to retain rights and autonomy.
What are my rights if I'm under guardianship?
You have the right to be treated with dignity, to receive proper care, to request a different guardian, and to petition for termination of guardianship.
Where can I report misconduct?
You can report issues to the probate court, adult protective services, attorney grievance commissions, or through our reporting tools.
What reforms does Eyes on Probate support?
We support increased oversight of guardians, limited overuse of guardianships, accessible complaint systems, and racial and disability justice in the system.
