The Launch of Eyes on Probate

Why this site was created and what we hope to change

Home Back to Blog

For over a decade, our family has endured unjust treatment in probate court, guardianship abuse, and systemic failures meant to silence families like ours. "Eyes on Probate" was born from the determination to expose that abuse and empower others.

This blog will document our fight, spotlight survivor stories, and offer critical updates on reform efforts. Thank you for being part of this journey.
