What Court Watchers Do

  • Attend virtual probate court hearings
  • Take structured notes on what happens
  • Submit reports documenting red flags or misconduct

What to Watch For

  • Denial of due process
  • Bias against families or disabled individuals
  • Perjury or false claims made by professionals
  • Excessive professionals billing from trusts or estates

How to Participate

1. Sign up to be a Court Watcher

2. Attend a brief virtual training

3. Submit your observations using our Court Watch Report Form
