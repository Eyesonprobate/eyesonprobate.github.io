From Caregiver to Fighter

What started as a simple caregiving role became a 15-year legal nightmare. The probate system that was supposed to protect our loved one instead enabled abuse, fraud, and exploitation. Judges dismissed our voices. Guardians ignored her needs. Trustees drained her resources.

I watched my mother cry as her rights were stripped away. I was forced to defend my sister from the very system that claimed to serve her. For years, we faced retaliation, defamation, and humiliation just for asking for justice.

But I never gave up. I documented everything. I filed complaints. I exposed misconduct. And now, through Eyes on Probate, I’m turning pain into purpose—to make sure this never happens to another family again.

This blog is the beginning of a movement. Thank you for reading, sharing, and standing with us.
