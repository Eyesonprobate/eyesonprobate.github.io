Ways You Can Volunteer

  • Court Watchers: Attend and observe virtual probate court hearings.
  • Public Awareness & Media Volunteers: Help spread our mission through social media and community outreach.
  • Administrative Support: Assist with email follow-ups, volunteer intake, and coordination.
  • Legal Research Helpers: Gather case law, statutes, and court rules for our advocacy resources.
  • Fundraising & Grant Assistance: Help locate and prepare grant opportunities and crowdfunding campaigns.

How to Get Involved

1. Fill out our Volunteer Interest Form.

2. Attend a brief orientation and training session.

3. Start supporting the cause in your area of interest!
