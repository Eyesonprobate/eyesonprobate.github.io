Our Vision

We envision a world where no individual is stripped of their rights, finances, or dignity through guardianship or trust exploitation. Our work centers on justice, accessibility, and accountability in the probate system.

Core Beliefs

  • Transparency and accountability in guardianship and probate law
  • Racial equality in probate law and every other system—racial equality period
  • Disability rights and autonomy for those under guardianship
  • Trauma-informed and survivor-led advocacy
  • Faith-based resilience and integrity in all we do

Our Goals

  • Help families avoid probate court through education and planning
  • Expose corruption and systemic failure through court watching and reporting
  • Reduce unnecessary guardianships and promote alternatives
  • Make it easier to exit guardianship when no longer needed
  • Push for legislative reform and oversight agency restructuring
  • Open our own law firm specializing in elder law, probate, civil rights, and disability rights
